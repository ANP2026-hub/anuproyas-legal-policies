Quick guidance for using the Anuproyas website and contacting support.

What is Anuproyas?

Anuproyas is a digital learning and services initiative. The website acts as a gateway to information about Anuproyas projects and offerings. Availability may differ by service.

How do I contact Anuproyas?

Email helpdesk2026@yahoo.com with your question and the relevant page or service name.

Are all services available now?

No. A service or product card may describe a planned or developing offering. Please check the individual service page or contact support to confirm current availability before relying on it.

How do I report a website problem?

Send the page address, a short description of what happened, and your device/browser details to the support email. Do not include passwords or OTPs.

Do I need an account?

Account requirements depend on the particular service. If a service requires registration, follow the instructions shown on that service before submitting information.

How are payments and refunds handled?

Do not make a payment unless the relevant service clearly displays its current price, payment method, service details and applicable cancellation/refund terms. If these details are not displayed, contact support before paying.

Can a parent or guardian contact you?

Yes. A parent or legal guardian may contact support with a question about a child’s use of an Anuproyas service. Please do not email sensitive identity documents unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

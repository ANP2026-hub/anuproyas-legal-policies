This page explains how to raise a payment or refund question with Anuproyas. Refund eligibility can depend on the specific product or service, the terms shown when it was offered, whether delivery or access has begun, and applicable consumer law.

Before making a payment

Only proceed with a payment when the relevant service clearly displays its price, what is included, the payment method and the cancellation/refund terms that apply. If any of these details are missing or unclear, contact support before paying.

How to submit a request

Email helpdesk2026@yahoo.com with:

  • The name of the product or service.
  • The date and amount of the payment, if applicable.
  • The transaction/reference number, if available. Do not send full card numbers, CVV, passwords or OTPs.
  • A clear explanation of the issue and the resolution you are requesting.

Review of requests

We will review the information provided and the terms that applied to the relevant offer. If a refund is approved, the method and expected processing time will be communicated in the response, subject to the payment provider and applicable requirements.

Service-specific terms and legal rights

If the product or service displayed a separate refund or cancellation policy at the time of purchase, that policy should be read together with this page. Nothing on this page is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

Need help?

For payment or refund questions, contact helpdesk2026@yahoo.com.

Important publishing note: Before accepting any payment, add a definite, service-specific refund window, eligibility rules, exclusions (if any), cancellation process and refund processing method. Do not rely on this general page as the only pre-purchase disclosure.

Information for parents and legal guardians about children’s use of Anuproyas services.

Anuproyas recognises that parents and legal guardians have an important role in a child’s online learning and digital activities. This page provides general guidance and does not itself collect consent or create a child account.

Before a child uses a service

  • A parent or legal guardian should review the relevant service description, age guidance and any service-specific terms.
  • Where parental permission is required by applicable law or by the service, obtain it before the child registers or uses the relevant feature.
  • Explain to the child what information is being requested and why, in an age-appropriate way.
  • Do not share passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information with anyone.

Information and supervision

Different Anuproyas services may have different features and information requirements. The service-specific privacy notice should explain what information is collected, how it is used and how a parent or guardian can raise a concern. Do not assume that this general page describes every product’s data practices.

Parent or guardian questions

If you have a question about a child’s use of an Anuproyas service, contact helpdesk2026@yahoo.com. Include the service name and a brief description of your concern. Avoid sending identity documents or sensitive child information in an initial email.

Requesting help

A parent or legal guardian may contact us to ask about information submitted through a service, or to request assistance with an account or content concern. We may need reasonable information to verify authority before acting on a request.

Important

This page is informational. It is not a consent form, does not represent that every Anuproyas service is designed for children, and does not replace any service-specific parental permission process that may be required before launch.

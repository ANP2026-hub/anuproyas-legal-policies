This notice explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you visit www.anuproyas.com or contact Anuproyas. The exact information involved depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

Information you choose to provide

If you email us, we receive the information contained in your message, such as your email address, name if supplied, and the details you choose to share. Please avoid sending passwords, OTPs, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Website and platform information

This website is published using Blogger and a custom domain. Google/Blogger and the relevant service providers may process technical information, logs, cookies or similar data as part of operating, securing and displaying the website. Their handling of such information is also governed by their own policies and settings.

How information may be used

  • To read and respond to support messages.
  • To understand and troubleshoot reported website issues.
  • To maintain, protect and improve the website and its services.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations where required.

Sharing

We do not intend to publish private support correspondence. Information may be processed by the hosting/platform providers needed to operate the website, or disclosed where required by applicable law. Do not submit information that you do not want transmitted through email or the website platform.

External links

Anuproyas pages may link to external websites or services. Those sites operate under their own privacy practices. Review their notices before providing information.

Children’s information

Where a service is intended for children, its specific sign-up and data practices should be explained on that service before a child submits personal information. Parents or guardians may contact us with questions. Do not send a child’s sensitive information by ordinary email.

Retention and requests

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain records or meet legal obligations. To ask about a message you sent or request a correction, email helpdesk2026@yahoo.com and describe the request. We may need to verify that the request relates to you.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website and services change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated review date.

This is a general website privacy notice, not a statement that every Anuproyas product has the same data practices. Service-specific notices should be added before launching features that collect account, learning, payment or children’s data.
